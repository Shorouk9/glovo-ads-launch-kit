# Glovo Ads — Multi-Market Launch Kit

A working launch kit for rolling out the 2026 Glovo Ads formats (audience segmentation, direct product links, video stories, product cross-sell) across 21 markets, built to keep launches moving through the Uber / SSW Partners carve-out.

Prepared as an independent proposal for the Glovo Partner Revenue team. Not an official Glovo document. Public figures as of October 2026; market scores are illustrative.

## What's inside

- **Context** — why Ads is the growth engine and why the footprint is being reshaped
- **Launch scope** — the four formats as one bundle, and a strict definition of "launched"
- **Market tiering model** — 21 markets scored on five dimensions plus transition risk; editable, with auto-assign and reset
- **Launch playbook** — 12-week timeline with four gates, readiness checklist, RACI
- **KPIs & exec reporting** — KPI tree and an editable executive one-pager template
- **Launching through the carve-out** — two lanes, one roadmap; what stays shared, what diverges, governance
- **First 30 days** — how the assumptions get replaced with internal data

Single self-contained page (`index.html`). All edits save in the viewer's browser only; every section has a reset.
